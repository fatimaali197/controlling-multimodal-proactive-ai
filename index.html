HUMAN SIDE / 04

ControlYour boundaries shape what happens next.

Configure perception, initiative, and support types independently. Allowing the system to perceive something does not automatically give it permission to intervene.

Try turning all signals off, limiting initiative to Silence, or disabling writing support. Then run a new illustrative decision.
