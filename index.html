Research context

Proactive multimodal AI can use screen content, speech, and mouse-and-keyboard interactions to offer assistance without an explicit request. This replay is adapted from the paper’s study of a public civic-data dashboard about fatal police use of force; the displayed values are manuscript examples, not live data.
